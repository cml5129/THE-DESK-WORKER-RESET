# Installing Exerset

Choose your operating system below:

## Windows

### Step 1: Download
Download **Exerset-Setup.exe** from the [Releases page](https://github.com/drjosh/exerset/releases).

### Step 2: Install
1. Double-click **Exerset-Setup.exe**
2. Click "Install"
3. Wait for installation to complete
4. Exerset launches automatically

### Step 3: Optional - Start at Login
1. Click the Exerset icon in your system tray (bottom-right)
2. Right-click and select "Start with Windows"
3. Done! Exerset will launch automatically when you start your computer

---

## macOS

### Step 1: Download
Download **Exerset.dmg** from the [Releases page](https://github.com/drjosh/exerset/releases).

### Step 2: Install
1. Double-click **Exerset.dmg** (the downloaded file)
2. A window opens showing the Exerset icon and Applications folder
3. **Drag Exerset** to the **Applications** folder
4. Wait for the copy to complete, then close the window

### Step 3: Launch
1. Open **Applications** folder
2. Find **Exerset**
3. Double-click to launch

*Note: On first launch, macOS may ask "Are you sure?" - click "Open"*

### Step 4: Optional - Start at Login
1. Launch Exerset
2. Click the Exerset icon in your menu bar (top-right)
3. Select "Start at Login"
4. Done! Exerset will launch automatically when you start your Mac

---

## Troubleshooting

### Windows: "This app can't run on your PC"
- Your PC may be running an older Windows version (requires Windows 10+)
- Try installing from source: see [Developer Guide](build/BUILD.md)

### macOS: "App is damaged and can't be opened"
- This typically means the app isn't notarized yet
- Try: right-click Exerset.app > Open (instead of double-click)
- Or try installing from source: see [Developer Guide](build/BUILD.md)

### Either platform: "Permission denied"
- Try restarting your computer and trying again
- Or uninstall completely and reinstall fresh
